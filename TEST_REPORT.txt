RESET v3.4.0 TEST REPORT

-----
This release was tested as an update from the previous local-state shape and as a clean state. Testing focused on the scheduler rewrite, Wake/Sleep separation, Week/Plan Day, cancellations/restores, meal ordering, manual moves, Gym polish, Money allocation states, Sleep duration handling, responsive layouts, migration and large local histories.

45 / 45 checks passed.
