RESET — START HERE

1. Upload this folder to any free HTTPS static host (Netlify / Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages / Vercel).
2. Open the resulting link in Safari on your iPhone.
3. Safari Share button -> Add to Home Screen -> Add.
4. Open Reset from the new Home Screen icon.
5. Go to Uni and edit each course to your real current week.
6. Go to Life -> Money -> Edit and adjust the suggested budget buckets.
7. Use Settings -> Export backup from time to time.

The app costs nothing to run on a normal free static host.
Your data stays locally on your device.
